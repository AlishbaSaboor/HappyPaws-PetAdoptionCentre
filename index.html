About Us

Happy Paws is a no-kill shelter that has found homes for over 2,000 animals since opening. All our animals are vaccinated before they are ever listed for adoption. Every morning starts with walks, playtime and fresh meals, and our volunteers spend time with each animal so they stay friendly and confident. Our vet visits twice a week for check-ups, and every pet is microchipped before going home.
